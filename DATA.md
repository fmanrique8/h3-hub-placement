## Public-data follow-up

The separate `madison-dane-v1` run uses the Census Bureau's 2020 TIGERweb blocks layer (January 1, 2020 geography) and Dane County's 2024 TIGER/Line All Lines archive. The block query selects four published attributes. Block population is a proxy for demand. The road parser reads only feature class and topological node IDs plus line geometry. The exact source URLs, versions, and SHA-256 digests are embedded in `hub_placement/metro_inputs.py` and recorded with the results. ## Postal ZIP codes, separate from the benchmark

ZIP codes are postal routes. Census ZCTAs are statistical approximations. A US ZIP-level study can produce approximate weighted point demand through the following import path after choosing the quarter and Census geography vintage. [Census ZCTA guidance](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/guidance/geo-areas/zctas.html)

1. Read the input as `zip,weight`, keeping ZIPs as five-character strings. Record the chosen quarter of the **HUD–USPS ZIP-to-Tract** crosswalk. Use the ZIP-to-tract direction so the allocation denominators are the total addresses in each originating ZIP. For example, `RES_RATIO` divides residential demand over tracts. Use `BUS_RATIO` for business demand when that choice fits the data. [HUD–USPS format and ratios](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/usps_crosswalk.html)
2. Join by ZIP and multiply demand by the chosen ratio. Check mapping coverage, missing or zero-ratio ZIPs, and weights lost to rounding. Give any renormalization or fallback an explicit rule and report its effect. Crosswalk vintages from 2023 onward use 2020 Census geographies according to HUD.
3. Join by the 11-character tract GEOID to matching-vintage Census Gazetteer tract representative latitude and longitude. The **2024 U.S. Census Gazetteer** is a reference for a compatible tract geography. Verify observed GEOID coverage for the selected pair of releases. Translate each representative point to H3 at the chosen resolution and preserve the fractional weight. [2024 Gazetteer files and formats](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2024/geo/gazetter-file.html)
4. Save the result and coverage log under a separate ZIP approximation dataset. Compare coverage and service metrics against point data where available. Tract representative points approximate where demand falls inside a tract, and address ratios approximate the distribution within a ZIP.

These links document the optional import only. HUD crosswalk files sourced in part from USPS and Gazetteer files are referenced to their agency download pages. No files, license claims for their redistribution, or ZIP-derived figures are included. Record the exact file version and agency use terms before adding these data to a separate run.

## ZIP archives

The narrow archive search covered personal Downloads, Documents, Desktop, and the existing personal site's release ZIP directory. Four site-release ZIPs matched the search. Their entry lists contained no H3, warehouse, facility-location, CSV, or GeoJSON results. Existing archives were inspected read-only and preserved. The current portfolio share graphic informed the palette by visual observation. The new figures were generated from the synthetic benchmark.
